更换运行环境
config.yaml
sandbox:
  default_gateway: e2b-v1
  gateways:
    - id: e2b-v1
      url: https://gateway.example.test
      api_key_env: E2B_GATEWAY_KEY
      transport: gateway
config.yaml
sandbox:
  default_gateway: k8s-v1
  gateways:
    - id: k8s-v1
      url: https://gateway.example.test
      api_key_env: K8S_GATEWAY_KEY
      transport: gateway
config.yaml
sandbox:
  default_gateway: docker-v1
  gateways:
    - id: docker-v1
      url: https://gateway.example.test
      api_key_env: DOCKER_GATEWAY_KEY
      transport: gateway
config.yaml
sandbox:
  default_gateway: cloud-v1
  gateways:
    - id: cloud-v1
      url: https://gateway.example.test
      api_key_env: CLOUD_GATEWAY_KEY
      transport: gateway

替换网关地址，并在 Grasp 服务环境中设置对应密钥。
